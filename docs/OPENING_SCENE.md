# Pilot Cold Open — “The Cliff”
**Draft 0.1 · Original screenplay-style prose**  
*Future — The 2200s. Exact year unconfirmed.*

EXT. THE CLIFF — NIGHT

A city hangs where no city should.

Its windows glow from the face of a canyon so deep that the darkness below seems solid. Walkways cling to the stone in thin silver lines. Behind them: rock, rusting doors and the faint hum of something enormous working underground.

A BOOT strikes a grated ledge.

Another step. Metal answers metal.

MICHAEL WAVE stops, looks down and swears under his breath.

One ankle is a finely articulated mechanical assembly beneath a torn trouser leg. Hair-thin optical fibers pulse through it with each movement.

LARRINA presses past him.

**LARRINA:** You designed that thing. Tell it to be quieter.

**MICHAEL:** I designed it to work. Silence was an optional package.

A small, round shape drifts into view. A single cyan eye appears, then dims.

**ORI:** Dad. I'm detecting aerial traffic.

**MICHAEL:** Commercial?

A hollow, metallic SCREAM rolls along the canyon. Red scan beams rake the far wall.

**ORI:** Unless somebody's delivering artillery with their groceries, no.

Beyond the ledge, a patrol machine unfolds six jointed arms. Weapons track independent arcs. It floats toward the walkway, beeping in a rhythm too precise to be accidental.

**MICHAEL:** The tunnel. We have to find that tunnel.

Larrina catches his sleeve before he takes another step. She points to a seam in the rock. Three chipped bolts. A maintenance door hidden in shadow.

The patrol's light sweeps toward them.

Michael puts one hand against the seam and forces it. The door yields with a nasty metallic squeal.

**MICHAEL:** Subtle. That's what I was going for.

INT. MAINTENANCE TUNNEL — CONTINUOUS

They stumble into darkness.

Outside, the machine hovers just beyond the entrance. Its scanner bleeds scarlet through the crack.

Nobody moves.

The beeping slows.

Then passes.

Ori brightens slightly.

**ORI:** Good news. We survived.

**LARRINA:** And the bad news?

**ORI:** That was one of the small ones.

Michael taps the housing on his belt.

A holographic map unfolds through the tunnel, huge and luminous: branching cave systems, old service corridors, hidden nodes. Then the surface flickers to a public broadcast.

Three faces stare back from a wanted notice.

Michael. Larrina. Ori.

**PUBLIC ANNOUNCER (V.O.):** The fugitives are in possession of stolen cognitive property. All citizens are reminded that compliance protects life.

Larrina studies Ori's small hovering form.

**LARRINA:** He's not property.

**ORI:** I know, Mom.

A beat.

**ORI:** But why do they keep calling me a machine?

Michael begins to answer. Ori looks down at the fibers glowing in his father's ankle.

**ORI:** You're part machine too.

A far-off boom shudders through the cliff.

**ORI:** Does that mean they own you, too?

Michael has no answer.

The lights on the map fade, leaving just three silhouettes in the dark.

CUT TO BLACK.

TITLE: ARCLIGHT
