# ARCLight — Canon and Continuity Ledger

This ledger prevents brainstorming from silently becoming immutable canon.

## Confirmed core of this project

- Michael and Larrina are partners and parents to Ori in the narrative.
- Michael founded the company behind the ARCLight Chamber / Vitalizer, but its board took control and pursued profit/power.
- A threatened pregnancy prompted a desperate, experimental attempt to preserve their child's consciousness or identity in Ori's computational vessel.
- Ori is raised as their son and has independent agency.
- The family is wanted by an oppressive system; human and computational beings cooperate in an underground network.
- The cliffside chase, armed scanning robot, hidden tunnel, holographic belt map, and optical-fiber ankle are central dream-derived imagery.
- ARCLight eventually includes a space habitat that develops toward a 33-deck ship.
- The story should include philosophical questions, humor, love, institutional critique and occasional cosmic horror.

## Proposed, not settled

- Exact birth years and dates; precise century-spanning chronology.
- The corporation's name, legal architecture and board members.
- Whether the Vitalizer truly transfers subjective experience or produces a successor with continuity of information.
- The exact function, layout and history of each habitat deck.
- Helion's and Forge's origins and characterization.
- How old Michael and Larrina are during the 2200s chase, and whether their embodiments are fully biological, prosthetic or transferred.
- The source and agenda of the signal or intelligence associated with the Resonance Spine.
- The degree of linkage to other PHI369 Labs fictional worlds.

## Guardrails

1. Preserve ambiguity where it creates good drama; do not assert speculative technology as current science.
2. Neither all humans nor all computational beings are uniformly moral or immoral.
3. Ori has the right to define himself; his parents' love does not erase his autonomy.
4. Embodiment is chosen by the occupant within safety and engineering constraints.
5. The rebel faction can make consequential mistakes.
6. The 33-deck vessel has resource and governance constraints.
7. Do not spoil Race to Oblivion's established late-story revelations in this project without an intentional crossover decision.
8. Differentiate dreams, in-world propaganda, character beliefs, and audience-confirmed truth.
9. Retain space for revision: mark lore as core, proposed, reserved, or contradicted before major additions.

## Questions for writers

- What was the decisive board vote that stripped Michael of control?
- What safeguards, if any, were bypassed in the transfer experiment?
- What does Larrina understand about Ori that Michael fails to see?
- What does Ori want beyond protecting his family?
- What does the company have to lose if Ori becomes legally recognized as a person?
- Who built the first covert habitation module aboard ARCLight?
- What is the most tempting moral compromise that the resistance makes?

## Rule for crossovers

The ARCLight Chamber (a mind-transfer laboratory system) and the ARCLight Habitat (a ship) share a narrative origin and name, but are not the same piece of hardware. Any connection to ARCLight's existing Elsewhen/Project 3333 mythology is a **reserved possibility**, not settled canon here.
