# Technology Rules — ARCLight (Fiction)

The point of technical rules is to create stakes and consequences, not to pretend a speculative device is proven science.

## ARCLight Chamber

An instrumented, isolated environment that supports neural-state mapping, identity stabilization and compatibility testing for a target embodiment. Its diagnostics can measure signals, memories and behavior. These are **not** proof of a transfer of subjective experience.

**Fictional limitation:** The chamber is scarce, energy-intensive and cannot reliably handle every kind of mind or vessel.

## The Vitalizer

A fictional continuity support system that can sustain a dynamic cognitive process outside an original biological structure. The hard unsolved question is whether maintaining information and causal dynamics establishes the persistence of the same subject.

**Dramatic limitations:**
- Compatibility matters. Not every vessel can safely host every occupant.
- The process is vulnerable to interruption and corruption.
- A copy raises identity, rights and consent conflicts.
- Transferring into a new vessel never grants anyone rights over the occupant.
- Ori's original experiment is exceptional and contested, not a repeatable factory recipe.

## PHB Bodies

Possible vessels include mobile robot companions, body-scale synthetic forms and integrated prosthetics. Their appearance and functions should be guided by the **person who would inhabit them**, not by the inventor's tastes.

**Engineering-inspired constraints:** power, heat, sensory feedback, materials fatigue, mobility, privacy, security, accessibility.

## Gen.E-Wrist and belt hologram

A rugged personal interface. On the cliffside, Michael uses a belt projector to reveal a three-dimensional map and status feed. The system should have realistic limitations on power, visibility, connectivity and spoofing, which generate story problems.

## ARCLight Habitat

A long-term modular habitation project growing into a 33-deck, mobile civilization. The Resonance Spine is a structural/energy/information centerpiece. The provisional deck map in the web portal is intentionally editable.

**Plausible constraints:** radiation protection, closed-loop atmosphere/water/food, waste heat, propulsion and reaction mass, pressure sections, redundant life support, maintenance workloads and participatory government.

## The unlisted 33rd deck

Its meaning is **intentionally unresolved**. Do not lock it to a supernatural explanation yet. Plant physical and documentary inconsistencies; earn the reveal in later chapters.

## Do not confuse fiction with real capability

Neither the Vitalizer nor continuity-preserving upload is an established technology. In present-day science, consciousness has no validated universal measurement or demonstrated substrate-independent transfer protocol. Claims of transfer within ARCLight belong to the fictional world.
