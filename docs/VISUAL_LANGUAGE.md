## Shape language

- **Ori:** A friendly hovering spherical core with an expressive single optic, paired with two orbital stabilizer shapes. A child, not a mascot. As Ori grows, his vessel may change by his own choice.
- **The Vitalizer:** A vertically oriented glass-like enclosure with optical loops and petal supports. It suggests an engineering mystery, not a magical soul extraction tube.
- **The Sentinel:** Weapon-bearing asymmetric drone with a single search optic and articulated limbs. Fear comes from surveillance and violence, not from machine identity.
- **The ARCLight habitat:** Central reinforced spine, modular mass distribution, rotating rings, external radiators and propulsion accommodations. The first illustration is suggestive rather than dimensionally engineered. Deck layouts should await a separate architecture pass.
- **The cliffside:** Scale, constricted passage, a small family against institutional force.

## Motion rules

- Animations are atmospheric and restrained: field shimmer, a gentle floating body, a slow scanner sweep and subtle parallax.
- No rapid flash, high-frequency strobe or jump-scare effect in the site portal.
- `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` disables decorative animation and automated reel playback.
- Reel progression is **opt-in**, with clear play/pause, previous/next controls and keyboard support.
- No music or audio is bundled in this rung.

## Canon and rights

Do not use protected character silhouettes, prop designs, film logos, existing dialogue or franchise music as production assets. Genre references guide broad pacing and tone only.

The prologue includes core story beats and *proposed* future arcs. The story is fictional. The Vitalizer's claimed transfer of subjective identity is not an established scientific capability, and the narrative deliberately preserves uncertainty.
