# ARCLight

**A story universe about family, freedom, identity, and the people who choose each other.**

> They built a machine to preserve consciousness. They never imagined what a parent would do with it.

ARCLight is an original speculative science-fiction universe and creative development project from PHI369 Labs. It moves from an intimate family story to a fugitive thriller, an underground resistance, a sovereign space habitat, and eventually stranger mysteries across time and reality.

## The heart of the story

Michael and Larrina build a laboratory and dream of a family. After a devastating pregnancy complication, they attempt an unprecedented experiment using Michael's ARCLight Chamber and the Vitalizer. A tiny flying computational vessel comes to life. They call him **Ori**, and they raise him as their son.

Michael founded the technology company that financed the work, but its board stripped him of control. When the company demands Ori as proprietary intellectual property, the family flees. In the 2200s, human and computational fugitives navigate cliffside tunnels, surveillance machines, and a world that mistakes obedience for peace.

The question is not merely whether machines can think. It is **who gets to decide what makes someone a person, and who is allowed to belong?**

## Creative north star

- **Heart:** Found family; the bond between Michael, Larrina, and Ori.
- **Adventure:** Fugitive routes, hidden cities, orbital construction, exploration.
- **Humor:** Character-driven absurdity and corporate satire, never cruelty toward loss or identity.
- **Ideas:** Consent, power, moral uncertainty, accountability, personhood, and self-determination.
- **Mystery:** Unreliable records, identity continuity, the forbidden Deck 33, and the larger ARCLight myth.
- **Horror:** The frightening unknown of consciousness and the universe, not a lazy claim that all AI is evil.
